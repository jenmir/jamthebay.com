To submit jam session reviews, email the webmaster.  
When sending reviews, please include:

- Location (web site preferred)

 - Dates and Times

 - Name of host

 - Skill level (open, pro, invitation-only)

 - Genres permitted (blues, rock, jazz, anything-goes)

 - Anything else relevant or useful for jammers! e.g. parking, door cover etc. 

We also welcome updates on existing sessions, and any news about ones that are defunct or on hiatus.

Reviews may be edited for content and brevity before publishing. 

Your email will not be published in the review, but we will need your initials to identifiy reviewers to our readers.

San Jose sessions 

Your jam session info hub

This section includes sessions in the near vicinity of San Jose from Sunnyvale down to Santa Cruz and all points south.


Five Points, San Jose

When: Monday jazz session 8-11pm

Where:Five Points Bar 169 W. Santa Clara St., San Jose 408.982.5816

Host: Rotating, expect tons of SJSU characters

Session FB link here. Send a review!

Good musicians, rotating every week. The crowd is SJSU-based, especially when the hosts went there. 21+ only. (updated Apr 2019 by DF)

The SJSU cats continue to gather with their various projects on Mondays. No word on whether they still welcome strangers. Calling ahead wouldn't hurt, at all. [Updated Sep 2024 by SJ]

Woodhams, Santa Clara

When: Mondays 8-11pm

Where: Woodhams Sports Lounge, , 4475 Stevens Creek Blvd, Santa Clara  408.247.3144

Host: Jimmy Tone & JSL Project


Weekly jams are back at Woodhams, which is undergoing something of a re-brand. Send a review! [Updated Sep 2024 by SJ]

The Quarter Note, Sunnyvale

When: Sunday (8pm), Monday (8pm) and Thursday (8pm) nights

Where: The Quarter Note, 1214 Apollo Way #409 (next to Lawrence Expwy), Sunnyvale  408.732.2110

Host: Varies

This is a blues-rock Jam night and a legendary place for guitar-slingers to show their stuff, and maybe less so for horns. Zoo-like atmosphere at times and rock-n-roll is always available on the menu. Please send reviews to let us know what's playing these days.

Jams still going per calendar. Venue FB page here, with an entry from Aug 2024 actually BEGGING for horn players to show up to jams with a free cocktail as enticement. [Update Sep 2024 by SJ)

The Sand Bar (formerly Fog Bank), Capitola

When: Pro Blues Jam Sunday evenings 7-11pm, other formats occasional Thursdays

Where: The Sand Bar, 211 Esplanade, Capitola  831.462.1881  

Host: Dennis Dove, John Michael, other rotating hosts

Dennis always serves up a great time, whether at Fog Bank, Little Lou’s or Murphy’s Law. He wants everyone to have FUN ! I did, and I support his other gigs. His crew is guitarist Johnny Cat, Admiral Stuart Horton, Oliver Harris. "Giving it back" to help me get better via the jams. (Updated Aug 2016 by MS)

Sunday sessions still going per venue site page. [Updated Sep 2024 by SJ]
