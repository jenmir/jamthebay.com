To submit jam session reviews, email the webmaster.  
When sending reviews, please include:

- Location (web site preferred)

- Dates and Times

- Name of host

- Skill level (open, pro, invitation-only)

- Genres permitted (blues, rock, jazz, anything-goes)

- Anything else relevant or useful for jammers! e.g. parking, door cover etc. 

We also welcome updates on existing sessions, and any news about ones that are defunct or on hiatus.

Reviews may be edited for content and brevity before publishing. 

Your email will not be published in the review, but we will need your initials to identifiy reviewers to our readers.

Helpful Links 

Or... those who walked before us

Several people over the years have tried to document the local current situation for jam sessions - a difficult task, due to the numerous and transient nature of most sessions. We salute those who have tried to keep up.



The Jazz tiP

A YouTube channel and companion site to highlight live Jazz happening in the San Francisco Bay Area, run by Aaron Bennett. Youtube channel has clips from current sessions as of 2025-2026. Note that these sessions may or may not be open; call ahead to confirm, if you're hoping to show up and play.



Craigslist open mics 

We don't list open mics on this site, but there are plenty popping up and coming soon to a coffeehouse near you.
