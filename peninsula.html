To submit jam session reviews, email the webmaster.  
When sending reviews, please include:

- Location (web site preferred)

 - Dates and Times

 - Name of host

 - Skill level (open, pro, invitation-only)

 - Genres permitted (blues, rock, jazz, anything-goes)

 - Anything else relevant or useful for jammers! e.g. parking, door cover etc. 

We also welcome updates on existing sessions, and any news about ones that are defunct or on hiatus.

Reviews may be edited for content and brevity before publishing. 

Your email will not be published in the review, but we will need your initials to identifiy reviewers to our readers.

When: Monday night jazz 6-9pm

Where: Max's of Burlingame, 1250 Old Bayshore Blvd, Burlingame (650) 342-6297

Host: Sunday's Cool Jam

We have heard for a while about a very good session that was happening at Max's. But we have not yet heard from anyone that has done it. Max's is the latest spot for a well-established session that was formerly at Savanna Jazz, then migrated to the Pioneer, then Longboards, and now here. The Hub, Redwood City

When: 3rd Saturdays free-form improv session 3-6pm

When: Tuesdays weekly Band Jam 8:30-10:30pm

Where:The Hub, 2650 Broadway, Redwood City (no phone)

Host: (3rd Sat Free-form) Darius & the RWC Groove Collective (host's site)

(Tuesdays) varies Tues FB RSVP link

These sessions are both newly established event and meeting at a space in downtown Redwood City.
Message from the Saturday host:The jam will be groove-focused, riff-based, and improvisational, for musicians and listeners/dancers alike (Funk, Acid Jazz, Latin groove, etc). It’s a Saturday afternoon hang, a jam session, a networking event, and a chance to enjoy groove music together. The venue has a full backline with PA (multiple DI feeds for bass, guitar, etc.), house keys, and an E-drum kit; the house provides wedge monitors for the stage. Mics are available for horns and vocalists.
Note: 3rd Saturday is an open-ended jam that does not necessarily focus on structure / cover songs, and is primarily improvisational. Please follow host's link to find the RSVP form / sign-up sheet for an upcoming session, and learn details of the session format. [Added Jan 2026 by Sat host]

The Tuesday session we know little about, other than "We have a full stage, PA, drums, keyboard - just bring your instrument and pedals! Or come and listen." (per FB) This session is upstairs - not downstairs, where the Open Mic happens.

Parking note: Downtown Redwood City paid meter parking runs until 8pm Mon-Sat.
