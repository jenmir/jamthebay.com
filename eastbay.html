To submit jam session reviews, email the webmaster.  
When sending reviews, please include:

- Location (web site preferred)

 - Dates and Times

 - Name of host

 - Skill level (open, pro, invitation-only)

 - Genres permitted (blues, rock, jazz, anything-goes)

 - Anything else relevant or useful for jammers! e.g. parking, door cover etc. 

We also welcome updates on existing sessions, and any news about ones that are defunct or on hiatus.

Reviews may be edited for content and brevity before publishing. 

Your email will not be published in the review, but we will need your initials to identifiy reviewers to our readers.

CJ's Saloon, Pleasant Hill

When: Wednesday Live Karaoke / Rock Jam, 8pm-11:30pm

Where: CJ's, 548 Contra Costa Blvd, Pleasant Hill, CA 925-825-3834

Host: Brooks Lundy & Friends, plus others

New session that started up in late 2024. FB page has videos of recent sessions. Not a blues session, but bring your rock-n-roll. And a little 80's pop might be accepted. Session link

Sign-up sheet, backline provided (guitar/bass/drums). Marketed as “Live Band Karaoke” but all musicians welcome. Lots of rock. [review by TB Jan 2025]


Vallejo Jazz Jam, Vacaville

When: some Saturdays - call ahead - 11:30am-2pm

Where: Community Presbyterian Church, 425 Hemlock St, Vacaville, CA 95688

Host: Patricia Kutza 707.319.4184 or pkutza at pacbell.net

Formerly at the Methodist Church. This is a jazz session reading Real Books.
Note from Host: All levels of musicians (including singers) welcome for non-competitive and supportive environment. You must bring instruments and amps if needed. Repertoire primarily based on Jazz standards fakebooks (and tunes/lead sheets provided in advance). Host requests a $10 donation, the full proceeds of which goes to support Church community outreach projects.

Jam still going as of March 2024 (per host), and still at the church. See Facebook session link for updates.


R Place, Livermore

When: Jam Night Sundays & Thursdays 9pm

Where: R Place, 2562 Old 1st Street, Livermore (925) 294-9693

Host: JD

This venue has a reputation for rock, rock and more rock. Send a review!

Session still going per a new FB page as of March 2025 ; no functioning web site. [updated by SJ Jun 2025]

Still going. Musicians and jam singers welcome. Expect more play time if you’re willing to be shuffled into random jam groups. Generally a full crew of regulars are available for each instrument to fill in as well. Always check in with JD to get in queue. Chosen songs are mostly 60-80s rock and some electric blues; the regular crews know those, but other types welcome. Not karaoke style. [Updated Apr 2025 by MA]

Brooklyn Sound Lab, Berkeley

When: Last Mondays free-form open jam session 6-9pm

Where: Brooklyn Sound Lab, 1201 University Ave, Berkeley, CA (no phone)

Host: Melissa Archie

Venue IG page Session Eventbrite

Non-traditional, free-form improvisational session for instrumentalists, singers, spoken-word artists and others. Brand new session held on the last Monday of the month, each month having a different theme. RSVP on the Eventbrite link for more information. This is more of a songwriting salon / get-together format. From host:
"Different focus or theme each month held at our Recording Studio Lounge. Open to musicians, singers, poets, dancers and appreciators of all genres and disciplines. This is an event meant to facilitate collaboration, connection and community via improvisational jam sessions in an intimate, cozy and crazy cool space - think Speak Easy vibes. [Backline notes] drum set, percussion instruments, keyboard and upright piano and a few bass's on site including a stand up. We've got bass amp covered as well. " [Added by host Sep 2025]

I attended Brooklyn Sound Lab's jam session in Berkeley... they were there, had drums, keys guitar bass. Pretty complete, and I had a good time meeting and playing with folks. [Updated Oct 2025 by J]


Eli's Mile High, Oakland

When: Monday Blues session (Unconfirmed)

Where: Eli's Mile High, 3629 M.L.K. Jr Way, Oakland (510) 808-7565

Host: Rotating

Blue Monday blues has returned, with rotating hosts. Venue has confirmed the session is still going, runs from 7-11 each Monday, jammers can sign up at 7pm and the host varies each week per calendar. This is one of the longest off/on running sessions in the Bay Area for all time, and we would greatly appreciate a review letting everyone know what they're up to these days. [Updated Oct 2025 by SJ]


The Bistro, Hayward

When: 1st Sunday jazz jam, 2nd and 4th Sundays blues jams
Where: The Bistro, 1001 B Street, Hayward 510.886.8525

Hayward's best-known live music venue has multiple sessions with various hosts. There is a jazz session first Sundays 4-7pm. The blues session second Sundays "Dave Walker Memorial Blues jam" is 3-6pm, current host Robert DaValle; fourth Sundays blues jam hosted by Gary Lamb. Some jam history turns up on FB searches. [update by SJ June 2025]
