To submit jam session reviews, email the webmaster.  
When sending reviews, please include:

- Location (web site preferred)

 - Dates and Times

 - Name of host

 - Skill level (open, pro, invitation-only)

 - Genres permitted (blues, rock, jazz, anything-goes)

 - Anything else relevant or useful for jammers! e.g. parking, door cover etc.

We also welcome updates on existing sessions, and any news about ones that are defunct or on hiatus.

Reviews may be edited for content and brevity before publishing. 

Your email will not be published in the review, but we will need your initials to identifiy reviewers to our readers.

Lucky Horseshoe

When: Jazz session 3rd and 5th Wednesdays 8-11pm , monthly blues session 1st Wednesdays

Where: Lucky Horseshoe, 453 Cortland Ave 415.282.3456

Host: ?

The schedule has shifted at Horseshoe, jazz sessions began March 2023. Unclear if the Wednesday R&B session is still going, but no FB update since 2019. Current jazz jam host has posted this information on Craigslist as of March 2024:
-----
For all instruments and singers. The jam is every 3rd and 5th Wednesday of the month. We play traditional standards from the Real Books. Charts will be on hand if needed but bring 'em if you want. We have a solid rhythm section with piano, bass, & drums. Singers are welcome.... BUT NEW RULES: 1 singer per set. That means 3 to 4 singers per jam MAX. You must show up early enough and sign up on the list to get a spot for that night. ALSO, and this is critical, if you haven't already proven that you can sing tunes and follow forms with the group then you MUST bring the chart you are singing. This chart should be IN THE KEY you are singing and THE EXACT FORM you are singing. It would be best if you send the chart via email then we'll have it in our list. If you don't bring a chart... you don't sing. Sorry but that's that. Reading words off of your phone does not count as a chart. Yes, there will be a keyboard there. There's no house amp anymore so guitar players bring a SMALL guitar amp with you.
-------
