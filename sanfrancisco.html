To submit jam session reviews, email the webmaster.  
When sending reviews, please include:

- Location (web site preferred)

 - Dates and Times

 - Name of host

 - Skill level (open, pro, invitation-only)

 - Genres permitted (blues, rock, jazz, anything-goes)

 - Anything else relevant or useful for jammers! e.g. parking, door cover etc.

We also welcome updates on existing sessions, and any news about ones that are defunct or on hiatus.

Reviews may be edited for content and brevity before publishing. 

Your email will not be published in the review, but we will need your initials to identifiy reviewers to our readers.

SFJAM at SFJazz


When: Monthly Monday open jazz jam session 7-9pm

Where: SFJazz, 201 Franklin Street • San Francisco 866.920.5299

Host: varies - check schedule
Session web link
Monthly jazz jam with shifting dates - check the Session web link for upcoming dates and Spotify playlist of pre-selected tunes. If you're looking for some place to get your Latin on, take a look.
[From the director] "All backline is provided: piano, guitar amp, bass amp, drum kit and also a Nord keyboard. When we do Latin-themed jams we also provide congas and timbales. With a few exceptions, the repertoire is played in the Real Book keys. On occasion the lead artist may introduce an original tune, and we anticipate that a vocalist may ask to change a given key to suit their range. We provide the playlists to provide jammers with recorded references mainly for inspiration." All sessions are free - players requested to sign up on the specific link for each session.
Some recent Youtube videos of the session available. Contact education director with any questions. [Added by host Sep 2025]
