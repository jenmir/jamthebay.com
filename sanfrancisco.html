To submit jam session reviews, email the webmaster.  
When sending reviews, please include:

- Location (web site preferred)

 - Dates and Times

 - Name of host

 - Skill level (open, pro, invitation-only)

 - Genres permitted (blues, rock, jazz, anything-goes)

 - Anything else relevant or useful for jammers! e.g. parking, door cover etc.

We also welcome updates on existing sessions, and any news about ones that are defunct or on hiatus.

Reviews may be edited for content and brevity before publishing. 

Your email will not be published in the review, but we will need your initials to identifiy reviewers to our readers.

Hotel Utah


When: (Irregular) Sunday night Pro Blues Jam 6-10pm

Where: The Hotel Utah Saloon, 500 4th St, SF, 415-546-6300

Host: Rotating
Host FB link Session FB link
Stan Erhart, Dennis Dove, Sean Donoghue and others are trying to get a session established here. It's been hit or miss a couple of times; strongly recommend calling ahead first. From venue site: "Free live music - open to all Blues musicians and fans! Bring your sticks, strings, harps and things - and let’s jam! Bring your appetite too, the new menu is delicious!" "The Utah Sunday Blues Jams provide a platform for experienced musicians to come together, network, and showcase their skills, while also offering younger up-and-coming musicians the opportunity to collaborate with more seasoned players. We’ll aim to create a vibrant and inclusive atmosphere that fosters creativity, musical exploration, and a sense of community within the local blues scene. The music showcased during the jam could range from danceable blues, swing and surf to be-bop, early rock & roll and country. Check the listings for each week's featured performer(s)." [Added by SJ Aug 2025]
